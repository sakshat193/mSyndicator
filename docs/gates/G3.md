## Access

- **h = 1**: one POST per status enquiry; no token, session or autosuggest call needed.
- No Cookie, Authorization, Referer or browser UA was sent (UA `m-syndicator/<ver>`). Responses are
  `cache-control: no-store`.

## Response shape (`livestatus/v2`)

- Envelope `{success, error, response}`; `response` keys `trainDetails, stations, metaDetails, lastUpdated,
  dates, disclaimer, feedback`.
- `trainDetails`: `trainNumber`, `startDate` (`DD-MM-YYYY 00:00:00`), `departed`, `terminated`,
  `currentStation`, `totalLateMins`, `cancelledFromStation`/`cancelledToStation`.
- `stations`: **halts only**, in route order. Per halt: `station.code`, `distance` (km string),
  `dayDetails` (`dayCnt` = origin-relative day, 1-based; `dayDiff` = relative to **today**),
  `arrivalDetails` (`schArrTime`, `actArrTime`, `actArrDate`, `arrDelay`, `arrived`),
  `departureDetails` (same with `dep`/`departed`), `haltMinutes`. Origin arrival mirrors departure;
  destination departure fields are null.
- **Actual vs ETA (A3 confirmed):** past halts have `arrived`/`departed = true`; future halts have `false`
  with `act*Time` filled. Unflagged times are ETAs, never actuals.
- `intermediateStations` (non-halt stations with `location` lat/lon, `distance`, `schArrTime`, null actuals)
  are all listed under the **origin** entry (49 under HYB for 12723, up to GNQ at 592 km; 9 under JAT for
  12426); order them by `distance`. `currentIntermediateStation` (a station object) appears under the
  last halt reached and names the last passing station (`PAR` under NGP).

## Things the parser must not trust (Task 10)

- **Future ETAs echo the schedule.** 12723 was 20 min late at NGP, yet every future `actArrTime` equalled
  `schArrTime` with `arrDelay 0`. MMT ETAs look like schedule copies rather than forecasts, so B2
  (Task 29) may be a weak baseline.
- `trainDetails.distanceCovered` was 0 for the running 12723 (587 km at NGP); use halt `distance`.
- `lastUpdated` for the not-started 12426 was `07-10-2026 00:05:00`, so it does not show freshness
  before departure.
- `dayDiff` depends on the request date; compute run-clock days from `dayCnt` and `startDate`.
- `metaDetails.details` is a reversed character list (e.g. "Current Station"); ignore it.
- Passing stations carry no actual times; position between halts comes only from
  `currentIntermediateStation`.
